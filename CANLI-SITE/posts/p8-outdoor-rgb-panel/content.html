P8 Outdoor Rgb Panel

Product explanation

2.1. Outdoor full color SMD Q8 5 scan module consists of red LED wafer、 green LED wafer and blue LED wafer packaged as a pixel and form a matrix，and then fixed to the plastic packages； 2.2 This module contains the driver chip and chip input buffer，connected to a LED display control system to display video、image and text message； 2.3 Through OE signal to drive red LED、green LED and blue LED driver IC，can form 43980 hundred million color conversion； 2.4 This module can be spliced horizontally or vertically，thus spliced into the kind； 2.5 Module features： • Use highlight LED and high quality plastic parts • High contrast display can achieve a good effect • Light weight easy to install、disassembly • Use constant current drive LED，light uniformity，low power consumption • Pixel pitch 8mm，total 40*20 pixel，each pixel consists of 1R1G1B
